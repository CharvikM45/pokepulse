# Collection card scans

Demo scans: Pikachu (Base Set #58), Eevee (Jungle #51), Mew (Wizards Black Star Promo #8), and Pikachu (Wizards Black Star Promo #1). Retrieved from https://api.tcgdex.net/v2/en/cards using the corresponding IDs base1-58, base2-51, basep-8 and basep-1 and their image fields. Artwork belongs to the respective Pokémon copyright holders.

The demo binder retains its illustrative numbering, finishes, and simulation rules. Scans illustrate the named Pokémon; they do not authenticate a physical printing or finish. Production variants use their exact image_url only when image_rights_cleared is true. An unknown card displays an unavailable state, never unrelated artwork.
